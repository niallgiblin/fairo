[y=0.108 x=0.039] A Big Muff clone by, very similar to the "47" Ram's Head BMP circuit, with several mods that allow for a wide range of tonal divertsity. The R2 input resistorhas been converted to a hi (39k) and low (390k)
[y=0.074 x=0.037] switch, affecting the low cut in the input stage. An SPDT switch in the second clipping stage toggles between bypassing the diodes, or switching to either Germanium or Silicon diodes. A 25k pot
[y=0.044 x=0.037] replaces the R8 low pass resistor (a variant on the AMZ tone/presence control) allowing a boost to the highs. The low pass cap at C8 was enlarged to 2.2uF to balance the low pass filtering. The large cap
[y=0.017 x=0.037] and resistor at C9 and R5 on the high pass side allow for less mid range scoop than the "47" Ram's Head, and more pronounced highs.
